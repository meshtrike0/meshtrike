HOW IT WORKS

Every phone is a postman.

Your message is sealed on your phone, then handed to whatever is nearby. Phones that can’t read it carry it until it reaches the person it’s for, for up to 72 hours. No tower, no server, no account.

  • Three steps
  • Delivery ticks
  • Every route
  • MeshGRID add-on

01 · THREE STEPS

Sealed. Carried. Delivered.

Keys are made on your phone and never leave it. Everything that travels between phones is already locked, so the phones in between only ever hold envelopes they can’t open.

  1. 01

    You write. It’s sealed.

    Keys live only on your phone. The message is locked before it ever leaves.

  2. 02

    Phones pass it along.

    Nearby Meshtrike phones carry it, sealed. They can’t read it or tell who it’s for.

  3. 03

    It arrives. Two ticks.

    Their phone confirms delivery. Until then the app shows exactly where your message is.

02 · DELIVERY TICKS

Never says “sent” while it’s still on your phone.

A mesh can be slow. That’s fine, as long as you always know where your message is. Every message shows one honest state.

  1. Waiting

    Still on your phone

    It hasn’t left yet. The app tells you why, for example that nobody is in range.

  2. Sent

    It left your phone

    Another phone or route has it now, sealed, and is carrying it on.

  3. Delivered

    Their phone confirmed it

    Two ticks only appear once the recipient’s phone sends a receipt back through the mesh.

03 · THE ROUTE PILL

Always tells you how your message travels.

It updates live under every contact’s name. When several routes work, the fastest one wins. When none do, your message waits safely and the pill says so.

04 · THE MESHGRID ADD-ON

Two apps, so the messenger never touches the internet.

Meshtrike doesn’t even hold Android’s internet permission. Everything that needs more than Bluetooth lives in a separate, optional add-on called MeshGRID. It adds Wi-Fi Direct for speed and a private internet route over the Nym mixnet, and it only ever carries messages that are already sealed.

MeshGRID Optional add-on

  • Optional. Meshtrike works fully without it, over Bluetooth, LoRa and QR handoff.
  • Can’t read your messages. They are sealed on your phone before MeshGRID ever sees them.
  • Only talks to the real app. It serves only a Meshtrike app signed with the same release key.
  • Internet route off by default. When you turn it on, traffic goes through the Nym mixnet.

Without MeshGRID, files over 256 KB go only to direct neighbours over Bluetooth.
