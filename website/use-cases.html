IN PRACTICE

Pick a situation.

For each one: what goes wrong, what Meshtrike does step by step, and which routes and features matter most.

CROWDS

Festivals & stadiums

Tens of thousands of phones overload the nearest tower. Texts stall and calls drop right when you need to find your people.

  1. 1Phones within 10–30 m link directly over Bluetooth.
  2. 2Photos and files switch to Wi-Fi Direct for speed.
  3. 3Messages to friends across the site hop through other festival-goers’ phones, sealed.

ROUTES IT USES

  • Bluetooth
  • Wi-Fi Direct
  • Mesh

FEATURES THAT MATTER

  • Voice notes
  • Nearby calls
  • Groups up to 50
HIGH RISK

Protests

Networks get throttled or cut, and phones can be taken. You need to stay in touch without leaving a trail.

  1. 1Pair with your group in person beforehand, so everyone shows as Verified.
  2. 2Turn on High-Risk mode: text only, cover traffic, instant lock.
  3. 3If radio is jammed, move messages camera to camera with QR handoff.

ROUTES IT USES

  • Bluetooth
  • Mesh
  • QR handoff

FEATURES THAT MATTER

  • High-Risk mode
  • Duress PIN
  • Panic wipe
  • No screenshots
OUTAGES

Disasters & outages

Storms and earthquakes take out power and towers first. Messages still need to get through, even slowly.

  1. 1Messages wait safely on your phone instead of failing, with a clear reason why.
  2. 2Neighbours and rescuers carry sealed messages as they move, for up to 72 hours.
  3. 3A paired LoRa radio reaches kilometres when nobody is close.

ROUTES IT USES

  • Mesh
  • LoRa
  • Bluetooth

FEATURES THAT MATTER

  • Delivery timeline
  • Public channels
  • Long range
REMOTE

Off-grid travel

Trails, boats, ski huts and remote camps, where there was never any signal to begin with.

  1. 1Pair a small LoRa radio to your phone.
  2. 2Short texts reach kilometres between radios.
  3. 3Back within range, photos and voice notes catch up over Bluetooth.

ROUTES IT USES

  • LoRa
  • Bluetooth

FEATURES THAT MATTER

  • LoRa radio
  • Voice notes
  • Disappearing messages
SHUTDOWNS

Shutdowns & censorship

The internet is filtered or watched, and ordinary messengers are blocked or monitored.

  1. 1Nearby, everything stays phone to phone and never touches the internet.
  2. 2For far-away contacts, the optional MeshGRID add-on carries already-sealed messages through the Nym mixnet.
  3. 3Where the mixnet itself is blocked, disguise bridges will make the connection look like ordinary web traffic. They are on the roadmap.

ROUTES IT USES

  • Bluetooth
  • Mesh
  • Nym mixnet

FEATURES THAT MATTER

  • Internet route
  • Bridges (later)
  • Sealed end to end
