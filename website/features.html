FEATURES

Everything a messenger does. Minus the cell tower.

Messages, voice notes, calls and groups that work phone to phone, plus the tools you need when the place you’re in is hostile. All of it sealed end to end, none of it needing a network.

  • Talk
  • Hard places

01 · TALK

Talk like you always do.

The everyday things, rebuilt so they travel well over Bluetooth and the mesh.

On my way to the north gateWaitingSentDelivered
Carried by the mesh

Messages that find their way

Text, photos, files and replies. If they’re out of range, nearby phones carry it sealed for up to 72 hours.

Voice notes

About 120 KB a minute, so they travel well over Bluetooth and the mesh. Up to 5 minutes.

Nearby voice calls

One to one over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct, with fresh keys every call. No tower involved.

Groups and public channels

Private groups of up to 50, plus public channels anyone nearby can read. In channels you get a different random name.

Swipe to reply

The quote travels inside the sealed message. Relays can’t see it.

Photos, files and video previews

Received photos are redrawn from decoded pixels, never shown from the original file. Files and videos are never opened inside the app.

02 · HARD PLACES

Made for when things go wrong.

Jammed radio, no one nearby, a phone taken at a checkpoint. These are the features for the days you hope never come.

  • Constant cover traffic
  • Text only
  • LoRa and Wi-Fi off
  • 24-hour disappearing
  • Instant lock
  • Constant cover traffic

High-Risk mode

One switch for hostile places. It also tells you what it can’t protect against.

Hold 3 s to wipe

Duress PIN and panic wipe

A second PIN silently wipes everything and shows a freshly installed app. Too many wrong PINs wipe it too.

Disappearing messages

From 5 minutes to 4 weeks. The timer starts when a message is first shown.

QR handoff

Radio jammed? Move about 1 MB of sealed messages camera to camera in a few minutes.

4.2 km

Long range with LoRa

Pair a small Meshtastic LoRa radio and short texts reach kilometres. Nothing else needs extra hardware.
