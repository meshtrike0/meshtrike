01 · HOW IT WORKS

Every phone is a postman.

Your message is sealed on your phone, then handed to whatever is nearby. Phones that can’t read it carry it until it reaches the person it’s for, for up to 72 hours.

How the mesh works
  1. 01

    You write. It’s sealed.

    Keys live only on your phone. The message is locked before it ever leaves.

  2. 02

    Phones pass it along.

    Nearby Meshtrike phones carry it, sealed. They can’t read it or tell who it’s for.

  3. 03

    It arrives. Two ticks.

    Their phone confirms delivery. Until then the app shows exactly where your message is.

04 · USE CASES

Built for the moments networks fail.

Towers get overloaded, cut on purpose, knocked down by storms, or were never there to begin with. Meshtrike keeps the people around you in reach.

Every situation, step by step
  • FESTIVALSFind your crewFifty thousand phones, one overloaded tower. Meshtrike doesn’t care.
  • PROTESTSStay togetherWhen networks are cut, nearby phones still pass messages, sealed.
  • DISASTERSCheck inStorms take towers down first. Phones and LoRa radios keep going.
  • OFF-GRIDGo furtherTrails, boats and camps. A small LoRa radio reaches kilometres.
  • SHUTDOWNSKeep talkingSomeone switches the network off. The people nearby are still there.

05 · PRIVACY

Rules that never bend.

Built into every screen of the app. There is no setting that turns them off, so nobody can talk you into turning them off either.

No off switch Why each rule exists
  1. 01Notifications carry no names and no content.
  2. 02No screenshots, no screen recording.
  3. 03Never says “sent” while it’s still on your phone.
  4. 04The app never touches the internet.
  5. 05Received media is never shown from the original file.
  6. 06Nothing reveals who talks to whom.
  7. 07No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.
