SECURITY

Sealed on your phone. Opened only on theirs.

There is no Meshtrike server to subpoena and no account to hack. This page says exactly how Meshtrike protects you, what it can’t protect you from, and how to check our work.

  • Rules that never bend
  • Four layers
  • Honest limits
  • Compare
  • Report a problem

01 · RULES THAT NEVER BEND

Private by design, not by setting.

These rules are built into every screen. There is no switch that turns them off.

  1. 01

    Notifications carry no names and no content.

    “New message”, “Incoming call”, “Missed call”. Never the sender, a preview or a photo.

  2. 02

    No screenshots.

    Every window blocks screenshots and screen recording, and the recent-apps thumbnail is blank.

  3. 03

    Never says “sent” while it’s still on your phone.

    A clock means it hasn’t left. One tick means it left. Two ticks mean their phone confirmed it.

  4. 04

    The app never touches the internet.

    Meshtrike doesn’t even hold Android’s internet permission. A separate, optional add-on, MeshGRID, handles Wi-Fi Direct and the private internet route, and only ever carries messages that are already sealed.

  5. 05

    Received media is never shown from the original file.

    Photos are redrawn from decoded pixels. Files and videos are never opened inside the app.

  6. 06

    Nothing reveals who talks to whom.

    No message paths, no hop counts. The app doesn’t know them and never stores them.

  7. 07

    No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.

    Nothing about you or your phone is ever sent anywhere. The build checks for it on every change.

02 · HOW PROTECTED

Four layers between your words and everyone else.

Protection starts on your phone and holds all the way to theirs. Each layer works even if another one fails.

01

On your phone

  • Database sealed with your PIN, stretched so guessing is slow
  • Duress PIN wipes everything and shows a fresh install
  • Panic wipe with a 3-second hold
  • Screenshots and screen recording blocked
02

In the message

  • Sealed end to end before it leaves your phone
  • Every call uses fresh keys
  • Disappearing timers from 5 minutes to 4 weeks
  • Received photos are redrawn from pixels, never shown from the original file
03

On the network

  • Relaying phones hold only sealed data they can’t read
  • No message paths or hop counts are ever stored
  • The optional internet route goes through the Nym mixnet
  • Notifications never show names or text
04

Who you talk to

  • Verified means you scanned each other’s code in person
  • A 30-digit safety number to compare out loud
  • If a contact’s key changes, sending pauses until you re-pair
  • Public channels give you a different random name

03 · HONEST LIMITS

What it does. And what it can’t.

No app can protect you from everything. Knowing where the edges are is part of staying safe.

Protects you from

  • Network shutdowns and overloaded towers
  • Relays and strangers reading your messages
  • A server being hacked or subpoenaed, because there isn’t one
  • Someone impersonating a contact you verified in person
  • Being forced to unlock, with a duress PIN

What it can’t do

  • Malware already running on your phone
  • Someone who sees your unlocked screen or photographs it with another camera
  • Locating a phone while it transmits, using radio equipment nearby
  • Contacts who choose to share what you send them

Not yet independently audited.

Meshtrike hasn’t had an independent security audit yet. Until it has, treat it as beta software for high-risk use. An external audit of the core and the app, a formal model of the handshake and envelope format, and a public report are on the roadmap, before we call it ready for high-risk use.
